Our Trips

Why visit Lesotho with Tlokweng Tours?

Lesotho is a beautiful mountain kingdom, full of breathtaking views, hidden gems and a rich culture.
The best way to explore it is with people who know the country and its people. At Tlokweng Tours, we make your trip easy and enjoyable by taking care of everything, from welcoming you at our visitor centre to providing safe transport and guiding you to some of Lesotho’s most beautiful places, including waterfalls, dams, caves and traditional villages. We offer comfortable accommodation, friendly local guides and opportunities to experience Basotho culture and connect with local communities. Whether you're looking for adventure, sightseeing or simply a chance to relax and enjoy the beauty of Lesotho, we make sure your trip is planned around your interests. All you have to do is arrive, relax and enjoy the experience.
