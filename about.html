About Us

Get to know us and the love for our country that inspires all we do.

Our story

Lesotho is a country built on rich traditions, resilience and a strong sense of identity. Its story is deeply rooted in the legacy of King Moshoeshoe I, whose vision, leadership and unity helped shape the Basotho nation.Inspired by this heritage and the beauty of our homeland, Tlokweng Tours celebrates the stories, traditions and places that make Lesotho unique. From our mountain landscapes to our cultural roots, we believe there is something special about experiencing Lesotho and understanding the history behind it. Our story is part of a much bigger one, the story of the Basotho people, and we are proud to be part of sharing it.

Our mission

To make exploring Lesotho a memorable and meaningful experience. Through quality tours, comfortable accommodation and genuine community hospitality, we aim to introduce visitors to the country's natural beauty, rich culture and heritage. We are committed to creating experiences that support local communities, celebrate our traditions and allow more people to discover what makes Lesotho special.

  • Safe and well guided tours
  • Respect for Basotho culture
  • Support for local people

Frequently Asked questions

Do you offer transportation?

Yes! We arrange transport for our tours, making it easier and more comfortable for you to explore different destinations

What should I pack?

Bring warm layers, a rain jacket, sturdy walking shoes, sunscreen and a hat. Mountain weather can change quickly, so it is better to carry too much than too little.

Do I need a guide?

For trekking, pony riding and remote areas we strongly recommend one. A guide keeps you safe, knows the trails and explains the culture along the way.

How will I get around?

We transport you in our own tour vehicle. It collects you from our visit centre and takes you to each destination, so you do not need to drive on mountain roads yourself.

Can I stay at your visit centre?

Yes. Our visit centre has rooms for guests, so you can rest before or after your tour. See the Gallery page for photos of the rooms.

What money should I bring?

Lesotho uses the Loti (M). Take some cash for small purchases in villages, because cards are not accepted everywhere in the mountains.

Do I need a visa?

It depends on your passport. Please check the entry rules for your country before you travel. Contact us if you need a letter or help with your plans.

Can I book for a group or family?

Absolutely. Use the booking form on the Contact page and tell us how many people are coming. We will help you choose tours that suit everyone.
